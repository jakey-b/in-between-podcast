之間 (In Between)

之間 (In Between) is a bilingual podcast hosted by students in the Graduate Program of National Taiwan University's Department of Foreign Languages and Literatures (DFLL).

Listen to conversations between professors about preparation for grad school in the literature and about the research happening at the DFLL.

The name, 之間 (In Between), reflects the bilingual nature of the department (between two languages), the standpoint of the listener (who may be between undergraduate student and graduate study), and the idea of literature being between the covers of a book. 

Episodes: 

  1. Professors Edward Nolan & Laurent Cases: Classics [EN]
  2. Professors Ya-Shih (Sophia) Liu and Yi-Hsin Hsu: Chaucer and Shakespeare [ZH]
  3. Professors Manuel Herrero-Puertas and Lisa Litovskaia: The Gothic and Fandom [EN]
  4. Professors Wei H. (Eric) Kao and Shan-Yun (Bruce) Huang: Modern Irish Literature [ZH]
